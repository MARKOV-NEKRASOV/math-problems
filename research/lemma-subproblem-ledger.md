**25 October 2026.** This ledger turns the current full-proof gap into smaller mathematical targets. A result is marked proved here only when its stated hypotheses and proof have been checked. Solving one item would be progress; the full conjecture still requires uniform finiteness of **distinct powered-value triples** across every all-prime hyperbolic signature.

## Source-audit target: a claimed cofactor exclusion

Mihăilescu, [arXiv:2509.18275v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18275v1), Theorem 1 prints a no-solution claim for `(x^P+y^P)/(x+y)=P^e z^Q` with nonzero coprime integers, distinct odd primes `P>3,Q`, and `e∈{0,1}`. The literal equation has `x=y=z=1,e=0` as a solution. An intended nonunit restriction might repair the statement, but neither a repaired theorem nor its proof has been validated here. Before using it in SP1, establish a corrected statement with precise exclusions and independently check the cyclotomic argument. The paper database marks this extracted claim *questioned*.

## Research discipline for these targets

For each proposed lemma, state the orientation, positivity, coprimality, exponent range, and whether unit bases are allowed. Separate an unconditional theorem from a conditional route, a local solution, and a finite search. A proof of any subproblem should be independently reviewed before being used in the full conjecture. Lean formalization of Fermat–Catalan remains deferred until a complete mathematical proof is found.
