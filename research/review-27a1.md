# Independent review: 27a1 prime-divisor filter

**Verdict:** The local congruence and the `q≡2 (mod 3)` trace calculation in `research/attempt-27a1.md` are correct under the stated 27a1-branch hypothesis. They give a necessary condition, not a uniform exclusion of the branch.

1. Freitas–Naskręcki–Stoll (FNS), arXiv:1703.05058, Lemma 2.3, says that for a primitive solution with `c≠0` and irreducible Frey `p`-torsion, a twist by `d∈{±1,±2,±3,±6}` has mod-`p` Galois representation isomorphic to that of one of seven fixed elliptic curves. The note explicitly assumes the `27a1` member of this list. For its `p≥17`, FNS Proposition 2.4 provides the irreducibility premise. The fixed curve equation `y²+y=x³−7` and conductor 27 are given just below Lemma 2.3.
2. At `q≥5` dividing `c`, primitivity and the equation imply `q∤ab`. For `E:y²=x³+3bu−2a`, `c₄=−144b` is a `q`-adic unit and `Δ=−1728c^p` has valuation `p v_q(c)`. The model is minimal and has multiplicative reduction. Every allowed `d` is a `q`-adic unit, so its quadratic twist is unramified (possibly split) at `q`, retains multiplicative reduction, and has discriminant valuation divisible by `p`.
3. For a Tate curve at `q≠p`, the semisimplified mod-`p` representation has Frobenius eigenvalues `1,q`, up to a common unramified quadratic sign. Equivalently its Frobenius trace is `±(q+1) mod p`. One can see this from `0→μ_p→E[p]→Z/p→0`. Because `p|v_q(Δ)`, the residual representation is unramified, as required to compare it with the good-reduction representation of 27a1. Its trace therefore satisfies `a_q≡±(q+1) (mod p)`, yielding `p|(q+1)²−a_q²`. This local Tate fact is additional to FNS Lemma 2.3, as the note says.
4. If prime `q≡2 (mod 3)`, then cubing permutes `F_q`. For every `y∈F_q`, exactly one `x` solves `x³=y²+y+7`; there are `q` affine points plus infinity, so `#27a1(F_q)=q+1` and `a_q=0`. Since `p` is prime, `p|(q+1)²` implies `p|q+1`; therefore `5≤q<p−1` is impossible in that residue class. This computation is valid for every `q≥5` with `q≡2 (mod 3)`.
5. FNS Lemma 6.1 excludes 27a1 for `p≥17` when `p≡1 (mod 3)` using the split-Cartan normalizer theorem. Its discussion after the lemma says the analogous non-split result is unavailable; Table 4 indeed retains a 27a1 entry for the `p≡2 (mod 3)` classes `5,11,17,23 (mod 24)` and says the table is valid for `p≥11`. Section 7.1 treats `p=11` separately. The note's source boundary is accurate.

**Precision suggestion:** State explicitly that an unramified quadratic twist can switch split and nonsplit multiplicative reduction, which explains the `±` trace. The congruence does not constrain the case `q=p` or primes `2,3` and cannot by itself bound `p` or `c`.

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.05058, Lemma 2.3, Proposition 2.4, Lemma 6.1, Table 4 and §7.1; local Tate-curve exact sequence as used above.
