## Concrete next literature-led test

For coefficient-one `x²+y^r=z^p`, apply 2605.02632v1 Cor. 6.1 only after verifying its two conditions `ℓ|z` with `ℓ≠2,r` and `r∤z`. For solutions failing either condition, record the complementary cases separately. Even when irreducibility follows for fixed `r` and large `p`, check whether level lowering plus newform elimination covers **every** surviving form. The explicit constant `C(1,1,r)` and base field `Q(ζ_r)^+` may grow with `r`; no F1 implication follows without controlling them. A successful F2 theorem must eliminate all residue, coefficient, and ghost cases for each fixed `r`, with no dependence on the solution bases.
