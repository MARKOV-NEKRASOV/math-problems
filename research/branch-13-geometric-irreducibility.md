# A F2 geometric attempt: use 2-adic potential good reduction to cover the `r|c` hole

**Status: restricted irreducibility implication; cycle-14 primary-source correction removes an unnecessary hypothesis. No proof of F2 `(2,r,p)`.** Fix an odd prime `r≥7`, let `K_r=Q(ζ_r)^+`, and let positive primitive `a²+b^r=c^p` have `b` even and `p` prime. The proof below uses Cazorla García–Koutsianas–Villagra Torcomian, *The generalized Fermat equation Ax²+Byʳ=Czᵖ and applications*, arXiv:2605.02632v1 (4 May 2026), §§3,5,6, **PDF theorem and proof text checked**. Source `arXiv:2509.23540v1` is used there for the 2-adic calculation; I checked its citation in Proposition 5.6 but not its original proof. This lemma extends the *scope of that paper's Corollary 6.1 argument*; it is not a new irreducibility theorem in its own right.

**G-13 lemma.** For each fixed odd prime `r≥7`, there is a threshold `C_r` such that, for every positive primitive `a²+b^r=c^p` with `b` even, `p>C_r`, and a prime `ℓ|c` with `ℓ∤2r`, the residual two-dimensional representation of the paper's Jacobian `J_{2,r}/K_r` is irreducible at every prime of `K_r` above `p`. Consequently, except for finitely many *distinct powered-value triples* with `supp(c)⊆{2,r}`, every such solution with sufficiently large `p` has irreducible residual representation. The printed Theorem 6.1 says **irreducible** whereas its Corollary 6.1 says **absolutely irreducible**; this note claims only the former pending an audit of its cited Billerey–Chen–Dieulefait–Freitas Corollary 1.

**Proof.** Theorem 6.1 of arXiv:2605.02632v1 allows **any prime ideal `q` of the fixed totally real field `K`**. Its four hypotheses on a `g`-dimensional `A/K` are: potentially good reduction at `q`; semistable reduction at every prime above `p`; GL2(`F`)-type for a totally real field `F`; and all geometric endomorphisms defined over `K`. Its constant is `C(K,g,q)`. Set `K=K_r`, `g=(r−1)/2`, `q` a fixed prime of `K_r` above 2, and `A=J_{2,r}`. Since `b` is even and `r≥7`, `v₂(b^r)≥7≥6`; Proposition 5.6 gives potentially good reduction of `J_{2,r}` at `q`, **without an assumption `r∤c`**. Theorem 3.3 gives GL2-type. The prime `ℓ∤2r` dividing `c` is a prime of bad multiplicative reduction by Proposition 5.2. The authors explicitly use that same proposition in their Corollary 6.1 proof to verify that all endomorphisms are defined over `K`. For `p>2r`, Proposition 5.2 gives multiplicative reduction when `p|c`, while Proposition 5.1 gives good reduction when `p∤c`. The latter proposition requires `p∤2rAB(Aa²+Bb^r)`, which for `A=B=C=1` and `p∤c` is automatic. It does **not** require `p∤ab`. Thus the four Theorem 6.1 hypotheses hold with `q|2`, and the constant depends on fixed `r` only. In a primitive solution, `b` even makes `a` odd; then `c` is odd, so the exception `supp(c)⊆{2,r}` actually means `c` is an `r`-power. The repository's independently reviewed fixed-output-support lemma (fixed inputs `2,r`, fixed `S={2,r}`, Siegel) supplies only finitely many distinct powered triples in the exception. Each fixed output `C=c^p≥2` allows only finitely many prime `p`, so these exceptions disappear above some `r`-dependent threshold. □

**Prior bound → new scope → exact gap.** The paper's Corollary 6.1 requires `r∤c` and `ℓ∤2r|c`. G-13 removes `r∤c` for the **even-`b`** portion by replacing its potentially-good place `q|r` with `q|2`. It has no explicit numerical `C_r`; Theorem 6.1's constant depends on `[K_r:Q]=(r−1)/2`, `g=(r−1)/2`, and the prime over 2, so it is **not uniform as r grows**. Irreducibility starts the modular method but does not eliminate the residual newforms or bound `p`; the F2 `(2,r,p)` branch remains open, as do F1/F0. The remaining `b` odd, `r|c` case is not covered by Proposition 5.6. Even in the covered case the construction gives no `log C≤κ log rad(abc)+o(p)` with `κ<2r/(r+2)`.

**Next falsifiable subproblem.** Determine whether `J_{2,r}` has a potentially-good place with constants depending only on fixed `r` when `b` is odd and `r|c`, or find a different asymptotic irreducibility criterion. Then explicitly list the residual level/newforms after lowering in the even-b case and test elimination. Abandon any claimed branch closure if comparison forms survive or if a required image/modularity assumption is conditional.
