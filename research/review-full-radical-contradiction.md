# Independent review: radical contradiction and support split

**Verdict:** The mathematical deductions in `full-radical-contradiction.md` are sound as conditional or necessary statements. They do not yield an unconditional proof. The Stewart–Yu estimate remains documented through a secondary source only, so any statement depending on its exact displayed form retains that source caveat.

1. **Threshold check.** The hyperbolic reciprocal sum is at most `41/42`, with equality at unordered `(2,3,7)`. Given `R<C^(41/42)`, a bound `C≤K_ε R^(1+ε)` yields `C<K_ε C^((41/42)(1+ε))`. The exponent is strictly below 1 exactly when `ε<1/41`, so `C` is then bounded by a constant depending on `K_ε` and `ε`. At `ε=1/41`, the exponent is 1 and this argument supplies no height bound. The strict inequality in the note is necessary and correct. Similarly `R≥c C^α` closes the argument only when `α>41/42`.
2. **Inverting the reported Stewart–Yu estimate.** Put `L=log C`. From `L<K R^(1/3)(log R)^3`, suppose `R≤cL³/(log L)^9`. Then for large `L`, `log R≤(3+o(1))log L`; the right side is at most `(27+o(1))K c^(1/3)L`. Choosing `c>0` sufficiently small gives a contradiction, proving `R>cL³/(log L)^9`. This is a valid asymptotic inversion *if* the displayed source estimate has the stated absolute-constant form. The project could not access the original Stewart–Yu article, and the note accurately identifies the secondary-source provenance.
3. **Support split.** For `s=ω(R)` distinct supporting primes and largest prime `P`, `Q_s≤R≤P^s`. The lower radical bound implies the stated lower bound on `P` when `s≤s₀` is fixed. The proposed extra upper bound `P≤L^δ`, with `δ<3/s₀`, would contradict it. Neither bound on moving support is established by the equation. If `s→∞`, `log Q_s=(1+o(1))s log s` by the prime number theorem, and `Q_s<R<C^(3/m)` implies `m s log s≤(3+o(1))L`. The note does not convert this tradeoff into a false finiteness claim.
4. **Fixed support and signature.** Fixed-`S` S-unit finiteness applies to `(A/C,B/C)` and, by primitivity, each rational pair determines at most one positive triple. Fixed-signature Darmon–Granville finiteness can be used to extract a diagonal sequence escaping bounded reduced signatures, but it cannot bound the infinite union. The note handles both distinctions correctly.

**Small precision suggestion:** The `(2,3,ℓ)` paragraph should specify that the proposed power-scale lower radical estimate has a constant and exponent gap uniform in `ℓ`; otherwise per-`ℓ` constants or a shrinking gap may not bound the union. This does not affect the main argument, which states the fully uniform criterion clearly.
