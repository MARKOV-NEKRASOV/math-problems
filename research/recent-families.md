# Recent varying-signature generalized Fermat papers relevant to Fermat–Catalan

**Search boundary:** arXiv Atom searches for “generalized Fermat”, “Darmon” plus “Fermat”, and “signature (p,p,5)”, sorted by submission date, through 23 October 2026 (Argentina time). I checked the primary PDFs below, not just titles or abstracts. This is a targeted search, not proof that no other recent paper exists. None of these papers claims to prove the full Fermat–Catalan conjecture. They are arXiv preprints in the retrieved metadata; I did not verify journal publication or independent replication.

## 2026: coefficient-dependent `(2,5,p)` method

Pedro-José Cazorla García, Angelos Koutsianas, Lucas Villagra-Torcomian, *The generalized Fermat equation Ax² + Byʳ = Czᵖ and applications*, [arXiv:2605.02632v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02632), submitted 4 May 2026. The paper develops a Frey-hyperelliptic modular framework for signatures `(2,r,p)` with coefficients. Its **Theorem 1.3** proves that for odd prime `p≥7`, `p≠11`, there is no primitive integer solution of `−5x²+y⁵=z²ᵖ` with `2|x` and `3|y` (paper notation `(a,b,c)`). Its Theorem 1.4 applies this to a separate conjecture about `5x²+q²α=y⁵`. This is **unconditional as stated**, but the coefficient `−5`, the exponent `2p` and local divisibility conditions make it a special equation; it does not settle coefficient-one `x²+y⁵=zᵖ` or Fermat–Catalan. Primary location: PDF introduction, Theorems 1.3–1.4, pp. 3–4.

## Late 2025: `(5,p,3)` with a conjectural input

Ariel Pacetti and Lucas Villagra Torcomian, *On the generalized Fermat equation of signature (5,p,3)*, [arXiv:2512.17845v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17845), submitted 19 December 2025. The paper studies `x⁵+yᵖ+z³=0`, including “ghost solutions” that obstruct standard modular elimination. **Theorem C** states that, **assuming their Conjecture 1**, a standard large-image conjecture for an associated representation over `Q(√5)` with constant `C`, there are no nontrivial primitive integer solutions with `3∤z` for prime `p>C` outside the finite set `P` of Theorem A. This is conditional and also leaves the class `3|z` and finite exceptional exponents. It does not prove uniform finiteness for the whole signature family or Fermat–Catalan. Primary location: PDF abstract, Theorems A and C, pp. 3–4 and 39.

## Autumn 2025: unit-coefficient `(13,13,n)`

Alex J. Best, Sander R. Dahmen, Nuno Freitas, *On the generalized Fermat equation x¹³+y¹³=zⁿ*, [arXiv:2510.12092v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12092), submitted 14 October 2025. **Theorem 1.1** classifies primitive integer solutions for `n=5` as trivial **unconditionally**, and for `n=7` as trivial **assuming GRH**. **Theorem 1.2** proves unconditionally for every integer `n≥2` that a nontrivial primitive solution cannot satisfy `13|z` (equivalently `13|(x+y)`). This is a genuine coefficient-one varying-exponent family result, but the remaining class `13∤z` is not excluded for arbitrary `n`. Primary location: PDF introduction, Theorems 1.1–1.2, pp. 1–2. The 2025 survey already records broad `(r,r,n)` results under `r|(a+b)`; this new paper provides a direct source and the explicit `n=5,7` classification, with the GRH caveat.

## Relation to current research record

The strongest actionable new source for the shared ledger is arXiv:2510.12092: add it as source-checked metadata and record exact hypotheses for Theorems 1.1 and 1.2. The 2026 `(2,5,p)` paper may inform modular techniques but its coefficients prevent direct promotion to a theorem about the coefficient-one Fermat–Catalan family. The `(5,p,3)` preprint's headline exclusion depends on an unproved large-image conjecture; it must remain conditional. A fixed or restricted family result cannot close finiteness across all exponent signatures.
