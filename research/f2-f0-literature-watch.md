## Quantified field-growth obstruction (checked derivation, not a no-go theorem)

For distinct odd prime exponents `q,r`, the H4 construction in Golfieri–Pacetti uses `K=Q(ζ_q)^+ Q(ζ_r)^+`. Coprime conductors imply the cyclotomic fields are linearly disjoint, hence

`[K:Q]=((q−1)(r−1))/4`.

Thus along F1 with fixed `q` and growing `r`, its *construction field* degree is linear in `r`; along F0 with `m=min(q,r,p)→∞`, it is at least `(m−1)^2/4`. Also `[Q(ζ_r)^+:Q]=(r−1)/2` and its discriminant is `r^((r−3)/2)`. This is a direct quantitative reason that fixed-field constants and fixed-level Hilbert-form enumeration do not automatically pass from F2 to F0. It **does not** prove that every specialization has minimal field of this degree (the 2026 rank-two paper gives special descents), nor disprove a different uniform method. Compared with repo's Theorem G, the HGM route keeps representation rank 2 while paying for a growing base field; Theorem G concerns Galois three-point covers and its genus growth does not transfer to motives.

## What a new theorem still needs

1. A uniform, explicit F1/F0 bridge controlling field-dependent large-image/modularity and Hilbert-form elimination (including ghosts), or an alternative method not requiring such control.
2. An actual numerical upper on `log C` strong enough for the planned squeeze, with *all* errors tracked in `m`, `R`, field degree/discriminant, and local conductors. Existing conductor exponents and Stewart–Yu/Pastén bounds do not supply this.
3. Source-audit resolution of the overlapping cases in Cazorla–Villagra Prop. 6.10 before using its wild exponent as a theorem.
