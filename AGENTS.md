# Agent workflow

For a Fermat–Catalan research pass, use four working agents on distinct mathematical routes where the execution environment supports them. Share findings in the problems and papers databases, and arrange independent review of proposed deductions. Agents may use separate Git worktrees for longer independent ideas, then bring useful findings into the shared record.

The conjecture counts distinct powered-value triples `(x^p, y^q, z^r)`. Keep the hypothesis `gcd(x,y,z)=1` and `1/p+1/q+1/r<1` explicit. A fixed-signature classification, bounded search, conditional abc argument, or numerical observation is partial work unless it supplies a rigorous uniform finiteness proof.

Do mathematical research first. Do not start a Lean formalization of Fermat–Catalan unless a complete mathematical proof has been found. This is the user's current preference and takes precedence over the implementation sequence proposed in `docs/architecture.es.md`.

Record exact sources, hypotheses, version and reading status in the paper library. Treat preprint claims and unreviewed extracted claims as provisional. Report blockers precisely; do not label an attempt as a proof without a complete justified argument.
